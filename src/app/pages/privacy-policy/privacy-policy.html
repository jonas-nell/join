2. Overview

Join ("we", "the app") is a Kanban-style task management web application. This policy explains what personal data is collected when you use Join, how it is processed, and what rights you have regarding your data.

3. Data We Collect

3.1 Account Data (via Supabase Auth)

When you register, we collect:
Email address
Password (stored as a salted hash by Supabase Auth — we never see or store your plaintext password)
Optionally: name/display name, if you provide one in your profile

3.2 Application Data (via Supabase Postgres Database)

Once logged in, the following data you create is stored in our database:
Tasks/cards you create (title, description, status, due dates, assignees)
Contacts you add (name, email, phone number if provided)
Any other content you input into boards, columns, or cards

3.3 Session Data

Supabase issues a JWT (JSON Web Token) upon login, stored in your browser (localStorage or cookies depending on configuration) to keep you logged in between visits.

3.4 We Do Not Collect

We do not use analytics or tracking cookies. (Remove this line if you add Google Analytics, Plausible, etc. later — and add a cookie/tracking section instead.)
We do not sell or share your data with advertisers.
